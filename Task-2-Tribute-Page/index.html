Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam 1931 — 2015
01 — About

A scientist who inspired millions

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's civilian space programme and military missile development.

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Kalam came from a modest background and developed a strong interest in science and technology. His journey became an example of determination, discipline, and continuous learning.

He worked with organisations including ISRO and DRDO and became closely associated with India's space and missile programmes. His scientific contributions earned him the popular title "Missile Man of India."

Beyond science and public service, Kalam was deeply committed to education and young people. Through his books, lectures, and interactions with students, he encouraged young Indians to dream ambitiously and work towards building a better future.
